# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning. Criticize ideas, not people. No harassment,
insults, sexualized language or personal attacks, and no publishing other people's private information. This applies
to issues, pull requests, discussions and community courses.

To report a problem, write to the maintainer through a
[private security report](https://github.com/pedromzn/Learning_Anything/security/advisories/new) (it is private and
reaches only the maintainers) or by direct message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove content and block people who break these rules.
